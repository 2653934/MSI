S3PL peak quality per section over the ten released training seeds (seeds 1-10), GBM (left) and CAC (right). Dots are individual seeds, bars the per-section mean over the ten seeds, and hollow diamonds the published per-section values, which are given to 2 decimals. Scores are S3PL's own in-job mSCF1 (metrics.json), not the fair-scoring evaluator used for the VAE comparisons. Panel titles give the collection mean ± SD of the ten seed-level collection means, beside the published collection mean. GBM: the published mean (0.496) is outside our seed distribution (0.319 ± 0.042, seed-level range 0.226-0.374); the published value lies above our seed mean in 8/8 sections and above every one of our seeds in 6/8. CAC: the published mean (0.601) is also outside our seed distribution (0.547 ± 0.038, highest seed-level mean 0.591), but only by a margin within the published values' 2-decimal rounding.
