records those changes so that exploratory decisions are not presented as if
they had been planned after seeing the outcomes.

These deviations preserve the central question while limiting its answer. In
particular, separate negative augmentation and penalty pilots do not exclude a
beneficial interaction. MALDIquant is deferred rather than added as a rushed
