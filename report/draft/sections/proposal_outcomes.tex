\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:questions}

The proposal asked:
(P1) how local spatial covariance can be integrated into a 1D VAE without the
overhead of 3D convolution;
(P2) whether a spatial-coherence penalty with physical noise augmentation
isolates true signal relative to spatially blind baselines; and
(P3) whether this framework improves spatially structured peak identification
over msiPL.
The penalty, augmentation and Bayesian tuning did not pass their development
gates. Fair scoring then showed that P3 depended more on the ranking step than
on spatial input. The report therefore answers four questions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=RQ\arabic*.]
  \item \textbf{Baseline.} Does the label-free VAE--GMM pipeline select better
        peaks than reproduced msiPL at the same budget? (P3, made fair.)
  \item \textbf{Ranking and representation.} Does the gain come from the
        attribution method (IG against correlation with the segmentation) or
        from the nonlinear encoder (VAE against PCA)?
  \item \textbf{Spatial context.} Does neighbourhood information give a
        consistent, spatially specific gain in the encoder, the segmentation or
        the ranking? (P1, P3.)
  \item \textbf{Reproducibility.} Do msiPL and S3PL reproduce, and how much do
        seeds change the results?
\end{enumerate}
The full proposal-to-outcome table is in \cref{app:proposal}.
